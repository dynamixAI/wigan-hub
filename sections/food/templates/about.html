About the data

Every rating on this site comes from the Food Standards Agency's open data for Wigan Council's area. The site downloads it every morning and rebuilds the pages, so it is never more than a day behind the FSA.

Ratings run from 5 (very good) to 0 (urgent improvement necessary). Councils carry out the inspections and send results to the FSA, so a new rating can take a few weeks to appear.

The data is reused under the Open Government Licence. This site is independent and is not run by or linked to the FSA or Wigan Council. If something looks wrong, check the business on the FSA site, which is the official record.
